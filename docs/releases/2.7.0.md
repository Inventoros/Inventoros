# Inventoros 2.7.0

This release lets you sell kits: a bundle goes on an order as one priced
line while stock comes out of its parts. It also redesigns the dashboard and
refreshes the interface around it, with search in the top bar, product photos
in lists and a proper dark theme. The full list is in the
[changelog](https://github.com/Inventoros/Inventoros/blob/v2.7.0/CHANGELOG.md).

## Sellable kits

- **What a kit is:** a product of type **Kit (Bundle)** with a component
  list, set up on the product form as before. A kit holds no stock of its
  own.
- **Selling one:** add the kit to an order like any product. The order shows
  one line at the kit's price; each component's stock goes down by its
  quantity per kit, with a stock ledger row per component that names the
  order. The kit's own stock is never touched.
- **How many can be sold:** the scarcest component's free stock (on hand
  less reservations) divided by its quantity per kit. A kit line and a plain
  line for the same component on one order draw on one shared count. A kit
  short of components is refused; kits are never backordered.
- **What can be sold:** a kit needs at least one component, whole-number
  quantities, and no variants of its own. Each component must be untracked
  (no serials or batches), not a kit or assembly itself, and not deleted. A
  kit that breaks a rule is refused with the reason, and its product page
  shows that reason.
- **Giving stock back:** each kit line records what it took. Cancelling,
  rejecting, deleting or editing the order, or receiving a return against
  it, gives back exactly that, even if the kit's components have changed
  since. Editing an order keeps a kit already on it at its recorded
  components; a kit added in the edit uses the current list.
- **On screen:** the order form, product list and product page show how many
  of each kit can be made ("Can make N"). An order's kit line lists the
  components it took, and pick lists show each kit's contents.
- **Reservations:** a kit cannot be reserved; reserve its components.

## Dashboard and interface

- **Dashboard:** Inventory value leads as the one highlighted figure.
  Replenishment sits beside a 30-day stock movement chart (units received
  and shipped per day, from the stock ledger; moves between locations, kit
  assembly and opening balances are left out). Each low-stock product shows
  on hand over minimum above a meter whose hatched part is the shortfall.
  Revenue this month compares month to date with the same days of last
  month, and only when last month had sales.
- **Search:** moves from the sidebar to the top bar. Cmd/Ctrl+K opens it
  anywhere, and "/" opens it when you are not typing in a field.
- **Product photos:** the product list and the dashboard show each product's
  photo beside its name, or its initials when it has none.
- **Colours:** in the light theme, primary actions, links and selection use
  a deep blue and pages sit on a soft grey behind white panels. In the dark
  theme, primary buttons are a solid blue with white text and panels stand
  out from the page.
- **Icons:** the sidebar, list headers and empty states use an icon set
  drawn for Inventoros, and every item has its own.
- **Plugin marketplace:** installs now trust inventoros.com's signing key
  by default, so signed plugins install from **Plugins > Marketplace** with
  no configuration. The official Shopify plugin is the first one published.
- **Product edit:** "Save and view" saves and opens the product's page.
- **MCP:** an `update_product` tool lets AI agents edit an existing
  product's prices, names, reorder levels and other fields (it needs
  `edit_products`; stock stays on `adjust_stock`).

## For plugin developers

- Filled surfaces in core (primary buttons, checked controls, the active
  page) use new colour utilities: `bg-brand-solid`,
  `hover:bg-brand-solid-hover` and `text-brand-solid-foreground`. A plugin
  that fills with `bg-brand` keeps working; switch to the solid classes so
  its buttons match core in the dark theme. `text-brand`, `border-brand` and
  `bg-brand-soft` are unchanged.
- The dashboard's `stockMovements` prop covers 30 days, one zero-filled entry
  per day, with `in`, `out` and `total` (net).
- `StockReservationService::reserve()` refuses kit products with an
  `unsupported_kit` error.

## Upgrading

One migration adds a table, `order_item_kit_components`; existing data needs
no changes. Requirements are unchanged. See the
[upgrade guide](../../UPGRADE.md#upgrading-from-v262-to-v270).
